What happens in a first consultation?

+

We spend the session understanding your goals, history, and lifestyle before recommending any product or program — nothing is prescribed before we listen.

Are your products suitable alongside medication?

+

Every consultation includes a review of current medication, and our practitioners will flag any interactions before recommending a product.

Can I join a workshop without a consultation first?

+

Yes — our Knowledge Hub workshops are open to anyone, whether or not you're an existing consultation client.

Do you offer virtual consultations?

+

Yes, alongside in-person sessions, we offer virtual consultations for clients who prefer to begin remotely.
